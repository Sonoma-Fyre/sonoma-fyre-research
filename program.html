The program

Come learn, bond, experiment, apply.

Three days built as a sprint, not a seminar. Every block ends with something you can use the moment you get back. Then a quarter to run it for real.

The three days

Four moves that change the business.

01 / Learn

Know what drives growth

Your real numbers, sharper positioning, and the few levers that actually move revenue.

02 / Bond

Build your cohort

Three days with owners who get it. The room becomes the board of advisors you never had.

03 / Experiment

Design one real bet

Build a launch-ready experiment on your own business. A new revenue stream, a new experience.

04 / Apply

Leave with a plan

A 90-day plan the cohort holds you to, and two shared events you launch together.

The quarter that follows

The three days start it. The quarter proves it.

Plans die when everyone goes home. So the work does not end on day three. The cohort runs its experiments and shared events over the next three months, together, and checks in every month to stay accountable for actually shipping.

Month 01 · Launch

Ship the first bet

Every owner launches their own experiment, and the cohort locks dates for its two shared events.

Month 02 · Run

Run it together

The shared events go live. Owners trade what is working and fix what is not, on a monthly check-in.

Month 03 · Prove

Measure the lift

Count the new customers and revenue, then graduate. Finishing the quarter is what unlocks grant access.
