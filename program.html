The program

Come learn, bond, experiment, apply.

Three days built as a sprint, not a seminar. Every block ends with something you can use the moment you get back. Then a quarter to run it for real.

The three days

Four moves that change the business.

01 / Learn

Rapid experimentation

Learn to prototype and test ideas fast, plus the numbers that tell you whether they actually worked.

02 / Experiment

Design your test

Pick one real innovation and design an experiment to try it on your own customers.

03 / Bond

Build the cohort

Three days with owners who get it, and co-create the two shared events you will run together.

04 / Apply

Leave with a plan

A 90-day plan, your graduation target, and two events already on the calendar.

The quarter that follows

The three days start it. The quarter proves it.

Plans die when everyone goes home, so the work does not end on day three. The cohort runs its innovations and two shared events over the next three months, together, with a monthly meetup to stay accountable.

Month 01 · Test

Test on real customers

Run your innovation live and put it in front of your own customers, who tell you the truth.

Month 02 · Run

Run the two events

The cohort runs the Sonoma Passport and Culinary Day together on their own spaces and channels, using the foundation the lab set up.

Month 03 · Graduate

Graduate, and keep going

Hit your success metric and graduate, then keep the group together with post-graduation meetups.
