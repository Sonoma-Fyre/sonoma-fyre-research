# Growth lab prototype (Sonoma Proof)

Concept prototype for the team's solution direction: a cohort growth lab for Sonoma small businesses. It is a fake-door landing page, built to test the value proposition with real owners before we build anything heavier, following Riemer's storyboard-to-prototype step.

- Author: Rohit Mishra
- Date: 2026-08-13
- Status: concept prototype for team review. Not a researched deliverable.

## What it is
A single self-contained HTML page. Open `index.html` in any browser. It walks an owner through the offer: a three-day lab (learn, bond, experiment, apply), a quarter of combined events the cohort runs together with monthly accountability, two signature events per cohort (the Sonoma Passport and Culinary Delight Day), a Sonoma Proof member seal graduates display, and access to grants, funds, and investors unlocked on completion. The "Request an invite" button is a client-side mock. No data is sent anywhere.

## What it is not
The names, counts, and example events (Sonoma Passport, Culinary Delight Day, a cohort of 10, two events per quarter, grant access) are concept parameters and illustrations. They are not interview or desk findings. Nothing here should be cited as research. Real numbers and outcomes come only from the team's field and desk work in `master-synthesis.md`.

## Source of the concept
The Innovation Narrative Storyboard (David Riemer, Berkeley Haas), sketched for this solution. Visual direction references lacocinasf.org for a community-forward, golden-and-blue earthy palette.
