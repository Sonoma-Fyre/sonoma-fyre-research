The unlock

Alone you have one audience. Together you have ten.

The cohort combines its experiments into two shared events each quarter, a passport and a culinary day. Every business markets to every other's customers at once. Each new cohort adds to a growing network, so the draw compounds. That is the thing you cannot build alone.

Each cohort builds and runs two signature events over the quarter. Two dates on the calendar, two reasons for a whole town's worth of customers to show up.

Event one

The Sonoma Passport

One pass, a stamp at every business in the cohort, a reward for finishing the trail. Each owner routes their regulars to all the others.

Event two

Culinary Delight Day

A single day when every kitchen and tasting room in the cohort puts on a special, and one ticket walks guests through all of them.

Other formats a cohort can pick from
Scavenger hunts Consolidated discounts Progressive dinners Seasonal markets
The mark

A seal customers learn to trust.

Graduates earn the Sonoma Proof mark to display on the door, the menu, the window. As the network grows, customers come to read it as a promise: a business that does the work to get better. Your membership becomes walk-in traffic.

The quarter that follows

The three days start it. The quarter proves it.

Plans die when everyone goes home. So the work does not end on day three. The cohort runs its experiments and combined experiences over the next three months, together, and checks in every month to stay accountable for actually shipping.

Month 01 · Launch

Ship the first bet

Every owner launches their own experiment, and the cohort locks dates for its two shared events.

Month 02 · Run

Run it together

The combined experience goes live. Owners trade what is working and fix what is not, on a monthly check-in.

Month 03 · Prove

Measure the lift

Count the new customers and revenue, then graduate. Finishing the quarter is what unlocks grant access.
