Wine grape trends: acreage, crop, and crush

Three different geographies, do not conflate: the Acreage Report is California statewide only, 2000-2025 (no reliable Sonoma-only figure could be extracted). The Sonoma County Crop Report is Sonoma County only, 2000-2024. The Crush Report shows both California statewide and Grape Crush District 3, the official Sonoma and Marin Counties pricing district, the closest available Sonoma proxy, 2000-2025. Presented in that order: acreage, crop, crush.

Source: CDFA/NASS California Grape Acreage Report. Estimated wine grape acreage, rounded to nearest 1,000 acres. Statewide only.

California Grape Crush Report, 2000-2025 (statewide and District 3)

Source: CDFA/NASS California Grape Crush Report. Grape Crush District 3 = Sonoma and Marin Counties combined (the official pricing district, not Sonoma alone).

California statewide crush tons, red vs. white

Units: tons crushed per year, 2000-2025
White Red
California statewide crush tons rose from about 1.5M white and 1.8M red in 2000 to peaks near 1.8M white and 2.4M red in 2018, then declined to about 1.3M each by 2025.

California statewide crush price per ton, red vs. white

Units: USD per ton, real (2000 dollars) and nominal, 2000-2025
Solid = inflation-adjusted to 2000 dollars. Dashed = nominal $/ton. 2025 CPI-U annual average is not yet published (data gap from the federal appropriations lapse), so the real line stops at 2024.
White Red
Real white price fell from 500 to about 392 by 2024 and real red price rose modestly, showing most of the nominal gain was inflation, especially for white.

District 3 (Sonoma/Marin) crush tons, red vs. white

Units: tons crushed per year, 2000-2025
White Red
District 3 crush tons fluctuated between roughly 60,000 and 115,000 tons white and 82,000 and 164,000 tons red, with no clear long-run trend.
