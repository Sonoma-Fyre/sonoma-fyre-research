## Live notes

### Why demand is down
Overall wine consumption is declining, attributed primarily to the post-COVID economy.
Discretionary spending has dropped, and wine, especially Sonoma wine, is often the first thing cut.
Tasting prices of $120 to $150 for under 40 minutes are seen as a barrier for many consumers.

### What Sebastiani has changed
Reduced the wine club allocation from 12 bottles to 4 or 5 bottles per shipment.
Adjusted the club discounting structure.
Increased social media investment.
Introduced a mixed pack of reds and whites so members do not have to choose individual bottles.
Added unlimited complimentary tastings for wine club members year-round, with one guest included.
