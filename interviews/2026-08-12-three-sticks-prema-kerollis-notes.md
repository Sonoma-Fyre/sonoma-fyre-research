## Live notes

### Event idea (team's proposed concept)
Worried the event idea might not work. Sees it as a lot of time and effort to put on, and too similar to BottleRock, so it might not be transformative.

### What wineries are already doing
Wineries are doing more interactive things.
Visitors are spending less when they're here, buying less than 750ml bottles and being more budget conscious, but spending more on experiences, including Enclos, a Sonoma restaurant, cited as an example of discretionary spend competing with wine.
Drinking wine on fewer occasions, and not collecting the way they used to.

### The broader pitch to a new consumer
Need to speak more broadly about wine generally to reach a new consumer, not just pitch a specific winery or bottle.

### Industry coordination
Wineries are working more closely together. Each AVA has its own vintners alliance (19 AVAs acting separately), and those organizations are merging to create a more aligned voice.

### Younger drinkers
Asked what the data shows for younger people: bang for your buck, volume for price, handles of Gallo, clearer pathways to entry.
Sees alienated children, referencing family picnics at wineries as a format that has fallen away.

### Dealcoholized and format experiments
Dealcoholizing wine is very expensive.
More single people now. Canned wine is very volatile as a category. Exploring the half bottle format.
