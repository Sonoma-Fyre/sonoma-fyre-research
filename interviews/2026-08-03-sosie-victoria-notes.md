## Live notes

### Size and profile
Small producer, roughly 1,500 cases. Employs a well-regarded winemaker; the wines have a distinct French-influenced style.
Visited on a Sunday. We were the only guests present.

### Why demand is down
Attributes the slowdown primarily to the economy and to political uncertainty, which she believes drives people to hold back on discretionary purchases.
The winery has only been open three years: a strong first year, followed by a steady decline.
Rising tasting prices, which she says are needed to manage volume and costs, may be compounding the slowdown.

### Foot traffic
Sundays used to bring three to five tastings. Now typically only one.

### What Sosie has tried
Investing in Google marketing and social media.
Built strong personal engagement; reviews frequently name Victoria directly.
One of few producers offering a GSM blend (grenache, syrah, merlot), which customer reviews single out as a draw.

## Interpretation caution
The Sunday drop from three-to-five tastings to one is a single operator's recollection at a three-year-old winery, so it cannot be separated from a young business failing to establish itself. It is far steeper than the county-level tasting-room declines on file and should not be used as a county figure.
